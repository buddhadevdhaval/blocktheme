Six steps from sample to report

Every sample moves through six quality checkpoints before a result reaches your patient.

Evidence-based test development

  • Every gene is vetted for clinical validity before inclusion in any multigene panel — if the evidence isn't there, the gene isn't there
  • A weighted scoring system guided by ClinGen standards ensures only genes with demonstrated clinical utility are offered
  • The result: appropriate, clinically useful information — and nothing that shouldn't be there
Learn about gene classification

Clinical review when the sample is received

  • A clinical review is performed as soon as a sample arrives — before testing begins
  • Ensures the right test is being run for the right clinical indication, reducing the risk of inappropriate test utilization
  • Provides clear next steps post-report and reduces the risk of a false negative result due to an incorrect test selection

Optimized quality control including DNA fingerprinting

  • Genetics experts review samples at multiple steps throughout the process — not just at the end — checking for accuracy, clinical correlation, and quality control
  • DNA fingerprinting is applied to every sample, both before and after testing
  • DNA fingerprinting can detect gender discrepancies and identify potential external sample swaps — ensuring the right result reaches the right patient, every time.

Independent data review & confirmatory testing

  • Variant calls undergo independent data review to improve classification accuracy and ensure clinically concordant results
  • When established conservative thresholds are not met, confirmatory testing of sequencing variants is performed — reducing the potential for both false negative and false positive results
Learn about gene classification

Expert analysis of complex regions

  • Ambry's experienced team and advanced technology enable consistent analysis of complex genetic regions, including promoters and pseudogenes
  • Complicated result types — such as duplications and splice sites — are interpreted with precision to provide clinically clear and actionable results
  • These regions are where many labs struggle and where Ambry's depth of experience makes the difference

Best-in-class reports

For every patient, the Ambry report goes beyond a result code. It is a clinical document designed to be immediately useful to the ordering provider.

  • Customized interpretation of results based on the patient's specific clinical presentation — not a generic boilerplate
  • Robust detail into the classification process, giving providers and patients greater understanding of what the result means and why
