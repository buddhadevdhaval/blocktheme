How It Works

Four simple steps from order to results.

STEP 1

Place Your Order

Order online via AmbryPort® or submit a completed paper test requisition form (TRF).
STEP 2

Collect & Ship Sample

Use the pre-paid FedEx/DHL envelope from your Sample Submission Kit to ship.
STEP 3

Lab Completes Testing

Ambry's lab processes the sample and performs analysis with unparalleled accuracy.
STEP 4

Results Delivered

Results are made available through AmbryPort® and your preferred notification channel.

Two Options for Ordering

Choose the option that works best for your practice.
Only orders from the United States and Canada are accepted at this time.

AmbryPort®

Our Secure Online Portal
  • Login to AmbryPort or create an account*.
  • Complete the appropriate fields and submit your order online.
  • Ship the Sample Submission Kit using the pre-paid FedEx or DHL** envelope.
* An Ambry representative will contact you to activate your account
** DHL envelope is optional for international orders.
Login to AmbryPort

Paper TRF

Test Requisition Form (Mail or Fax)
  • Download a copy of the test requisition form or use the form provided in your Sample Submission Kit.
  • Complete all fields carefully to avoid delays in processing.
  • Ship the Sample Submission Kit, with the completed TRF, using the pre-paid FedEx or DHL* envelope.
  • If using insurance billing, please provide clinical documentation and copies of the insurance card.
* DHL envelope is optional for international orders.
